## 0.0.1
 
- Initial release of the Alter Design System for Flutter.
- Curated typography scale supporting Geist and Instrument Serif font families.
- 234 Figma color swatches (`AlterColors`) and semantic tokens (`AlterSemanticTokens`).
- Reusable UI components: `ApplicationHeader`, `AlterBottomNavigationBar`, `BottomNavigationBarAction`, `ButtonText`, `ButtonIcon`, `ButtonIconGhost`, `ButtonGraphicText`, `ButtonGraphicImage`, `Search`, `Checkbox`, `ToggleIcon`, `ListItem`, and `Pill`.
- Interactive Widgetbook showcase and workbench in `example/`.
